About Project

Shadow AI Sentinel checks text for emails, phone numbers, API keys and passwords before you share it with an AI chatbot, rates the risk, and can redact the sensitive values.

Tech: Python, Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
Tables: Users, Scans, DetectionCategories, ScanFindings, SecurityRules.

Risk rules: High = API key or credential found. Medium = phone number found. Low = only email or nothing.
